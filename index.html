01 / PROFILE

Experimental depth.
Computational perspective.

I study how processing changes the microstructure, mechanical response and fatigue life of materials. Alongside experimental research, I build data systems that make engineering records structured, traceable and useful.

My doctoral research at SASTRA Deemed University examined heat treatment, deep cryogenic treatment and equal-channel angular pressing of Al 6063. My current work connects fatigue reliability, manufacturing-data infrastructure and digital engineering education.

02 / RESEARCH

Processing. Structure. Performance.

Experimental foundations across fatigue, metallurgy and composite processing.

PHD · SASTRA · 2018–2025

Fatigue behaviour of Al 6063

Heat-treated, deep cryogenic treated and ECAP-processed conditions; comparison of 90° and 120° channel-angle dies.

Servo-hydraulic low-cycle fatigue, tensile and hardness testing, microscopy, XRD and fractography connect processing routes to cyclic response and failure.

Thesis title

Fatigue Behaviour of Al 6063 alloy in Heat Treated, Deep Cryogenic Treated and Equal Channel Angular Pressing (ECAP) Processed Conditions

Supervisor: Dr. S. Ramesh Kumar

MTECH · ADVANCED MANUFACTURING · 2016–2018

Aluminium–graphene nanoplatelet composites

Powder-metallurgy fabrication with 2, 3 and 4 wt% GNP reinforcement, followed by sintering, hot extrusion and mechanical / tribological characterisation.

Investigated dispersion, interfacial bonding and the balance between strength, ductility and wear resistance.
